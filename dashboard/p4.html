What it does

A ~10-minute rerun after any Mesa, kernel, oneAPI / Level Zero, llama.cpp or LM Studio update. It compares SYCL (flash attention on, micro-batch 512) against the Phase 2 baseline and flags anything that moved more than 5%. The first run also does the micro-batch check: do Qwen and Muse (and Vulkan) gain from -ub 2048 the way Gemma did? Rule: +10% on pp16384 without costing more than 5% decode makes it that model's default.
