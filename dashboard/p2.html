The question

Same commit (llama.cpp 8bb3cf0d2), same GGUFs, same tests: is Intel's SYCL backend faster than Vulkan on the B65? Vulkan decode sits at ~55% of the card's bandwidth ceiling. Rule: SYCL ≥10% faster on decode → SYCL becomes the default engine; within ±10% or slower → keep Vulkan. Vulkan runs with flash attention off (Phase 0.5); SYCL is tested with it off and on.
